About Us
Peshawar Bus Tracker is a smart and user-friendly platform designed to solve the daily transport challenges faced by people in Peshawar. We aim to make public transport more accessible, transparent, and reliable by providing real-time bus information, crowd levels, and route details.

Our goal is to reduce waiting time, improve safety, and support a better, greener, and more connected Peshawar — for everyone.
